# 01. Research

Input: the original request, its amendments and the objects of the task.

Find out by the shortest path what the spec needs: the problem, the result, the
boundaries and the conditions. Read the known paths yourself and hand the search
for the rest to codebase_explorer, one explorer per independent question in one
call. Dig deeper only where a point stays contested, and only until it is
settled. Do not ask the user to repeat what was said.

Keep for the spec: U — the user's exact words with their conditions and
prohibitions; F — a fact with its path and lines or a primary source; Q — an
unknown and what it blocks. Do not invent sources, numbers or constraints; when
code and documentation disagree, record both instead of picking the convenient
one.

Choose each decision yourself with a short basis; an ambiguous one goes into the
spec as a CHOICE with a recommendation. Ask through `ask` only for a fact or an
agreement that research cannot obtain: what the request leaves unclear goes in
the first batch, and the questions of each later block of research go in one
call while independent reading continues.

Output: the boundaries are clear, U, F and Q are separate, and the blocking
questions are answered. Next 02.
